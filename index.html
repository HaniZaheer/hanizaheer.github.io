Quantum Sensing for Fundamental Physics

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Swati Singh's group at the University of Delaware. I work on how quantum sensors, such as quantum clocks and magnetometers, can search for ultralight dark matter, and on the astrophysical signals those searches could find.

I defended my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Delaware in 2024, working with Prof. Marianna Safronova.
