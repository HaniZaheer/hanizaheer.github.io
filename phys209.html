PHYS 209 Aspects of Modern Physics

Module 4: Qubits. An introduction to two-level quantum systems for undergraduates, built on the matrix formulation of two-level systems and following it from the Bloch sphere to quantum sensing, entanglement, communication, algorithms, decoherence and error correction.
